Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026. Please read this policy carefully — it explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

1. Introduction

Welcome to STEMBridge Tools ("we", "us", or "our"), accessible at stembridgetools.netlify.app. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use our website, how we use it, and what choices you have.

By using STEMBridge Tools, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the website.

2. Information We Collect

2a. Information You Provide Voluntarily

When you submit the contact form on our website, you provide us with your name, email address, and the content of your message. We use this information solely to respond to your enquiry. We do not add you to any mailing list without your explicit consent.

2b. Information Collected Automatically

Like most websites, STEMBridge Tools collects certain information automatically when you visit. This includes your IP address, browser type, operating system, the pages you visit, the time and date of your visit, and the page you came from. This data is used in aggregate form to understand how visitors use the site and to improve it. We do not use this data to identify individual users.

2c. Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites you visit. STEMBridge Tools uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Analytics cookies — to understand how visitors interact with the website (pages viewed, time spent, etc.).
  • Advertising cookies — placed by Google AdSense to display relevant advertisements (see Section 4 below).

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to refuse cookies, delete existing cookies, or be notified when a cookie is set. Note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of some parts of this website.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Operate and improve the STEMBridge Tools website and its tools.
  • Respond to messages you send us through the contact form.
  • Understand how users navigate the site so we can make it more useful.
  • Display advertising through Google AdSense (see Section 4).

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for their own marketing purposes.

4. Google AdSense and Advertising

STEMBridge Tools uses Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense is an advertising service provided by Google LLC. As a third-party vendor, Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website and other sites on the internet.

DoubleClick Cookie

Google's use of the DoubleClick cookie enables Google and its partners to serve ads to users based on their visit to STEMBridge Tools and/or other websites on the internet. Users may opt out of the use of the DoubleClick cookie for interest-based advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

How Google Uses Advertising Data

Google may use data from its advertising cookie to personalise the ads shown to you. This is governed by Google's own privacy policy, which you can read at policies.google.com/privacy. We have no access to or control over the cookies that Google places on your device for advertising purposes.

Opting Out of Personalised Ads

You can opt out of personalised advertising from Google and other participating advertisers by visiting the Digital Advertising Alliance opt-out page or the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page. You can also manage your Google ad preferences at myaccount.google.com.

5. Google Analytics

We may use Google Analytics to collect information about how visitors use our website. Google Analytics uses cookies to gather data such as how often users visit the site, what pages they visit, and what other sites they used prior to arriving. We use this data to improve the website. Google Analytics collects only the IP address assigned to you on the date you visit, not your name or other identifying information.

You can prevent Google Analytics from collecting your data by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

6. EmailJS

Our contact form uses EmailJS, a third-party email delivery service, to send your message to our inbox. When you submit the contact form, the information you provide (name, email address, subject, and message) is transmitted through EmailJS's servers. EmailJS does not store your message beyond what is needed to deliver it. Please review EmailJS's Privacy Policy for further details.

7. Children's Privacy

STEMBridge Tools is designed to be used by students, including those under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13 through any means other than the voluntary contact form. The contact form is intended for students, teachers, and parents. If you believe a child under 13 has submitted personal information through our contact form without parental consent, please contact us at thestembridgechannel@gmail.com and we will delete it promptly.

We display advertising through Google AdSense. Google has policies in place to restrict personalised advertising to users under 13. We comply with Google's policies and applicable law in this regard.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites for educational purposes. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee the absolute security of information transmitted to or from our website.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the website after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.

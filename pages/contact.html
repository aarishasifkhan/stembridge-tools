Frequently Asked Questions

Are the tools really free?

Yes — completely free, with no account or sign-up required. You open the page and start solving. We keep the tools free by displaying ads, which helps cover our running costs.

Can I use STEMBridge Tools on my phone?

Absolutely. The entire website is mobile-first and works smoothly on any screen size — phone, tablet, or desktop.

I found a mistake in a solution. What should I do?

Please let us know using the contact form above. Tell us which tool, which equation or formula, and what you think the correct answer should be. We take accuracy seriously and will fix errors quickly.

Can you add a specific formula or reaction?

Yes — and we welcome those requests. Use the contact form and tell us exactly which topic you need. We prioritise additions based on what students and teachers ask for most.

Is my data safe when I use this form?

We use EmailJS to send your message — your data goes directly to our inbox and is not stored on any third-party server beyond what email delivery requires. We never sell or share your contact details. Read our Privacy Policy for full details.
